THE SWARM ECOSYSTEM

SWARM Wallet

Your wallet, wherever you are. Choose your platform to get started.

SWARM mainnet was restarted on 2 October 2026

SWARM Wallet 0.1.0-mainnet.10 for Windows, macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Linux is the version for the restarted network; Android follows. Earlier builds (desktop 0.1.0-mainnet.9, Android 0.2.0-mainnet.4) were made for the first chain and cannot connect. Install the new version over the old one: your addresses and recovery phrase stay the same, the wallet keeps a backup copy of its file and starts again from the new chain, where balances begin at zero because the coins of the first chain do not exist on it.

TESTNET

The public testnet is still running.

SWARM mainnet is the real network, and everything above is a mainnet build. The public testnet stays online for testing: it is a different chain, its addresses start swarm1…, and its coins have no value and never will. Only install one of these if you know you want the testnet.
