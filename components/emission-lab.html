Exact emission schedule and calculation rules

At year zero, height and issuance are zero. The initial 6.25 SWM reward starts at block 1. The first halving is at height 1,679,999; later halvings are 1,680,000 blocks apart. Each fund allocation is rounded down in atomic units and the miner receives the remainder. The displayed first 24 target years divide exactly into the stated shares.
