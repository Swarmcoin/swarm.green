Which address should I use?

A native mainnet address from SWARM Wallet, beginning with swm1, s1 or s3. A testnet address beginning with swarm1 is not accepted. Do not use a Base, BNB Smart Chain or Solana address.

Already joined, or using another device?

Your original browser restores your place and invite link using its saved key. Re-entering the same email and SWARM address on another device confirms your place, but does not recover that private key or reveal your invite link.

To remove your entry, use the original browser or a removal link from a project email. You can also write to swarmofficial@atomicmail.io from the email address you joined with.

What emails will I receive?

When email delivery is enabled, we ask you to confirm your address and tell you when your early access starts. Project news is separate and optional. You can stop project news while keeping your waiting-list place.

Read what we store and for how long.

How does the closed start work?

SWARM mainnet began on 2 October 2026. Until the scheduled waiting-list opening on 31 October at 15:42 UTC, only project machines mine. Public mining is scheduled for 1 November at 15:42 UTC.

The project receives mining rewards during this period, alongside the published fund allocations. Read the network rules and allocations and verify the chain.

What if the service is unavailable?

An unavailable count or leaderboard does not remove your entry. Keep your original browser data. If signup cannot be reached, wait and try again; the form shows the service response rather than inventing a place.
