ONE THING THAT MATTERS

Back up your recovery phrase offline.

Write the phrase down on paper and store it somewhere safe. Do not photograph it, put it in a password manager you do not control, or type it into a website that asks you to “verify” it.

Anyone who has the phrase has the coins. If you lose it, nobody — including us — can recover your wallet for you. Treat anyone asking for your phrase as a thief.
