NATIVE COIN · PUBLIC TOKENS

How to get SWM.

Two ways: mine it on SWARM mainnet when public mining opens, or swap for the SWM token on Base, BNB Smart Chain or Solana. There was no sale and there is no VC round. Nobody promises you a price.

NATIVE SWARM · PROOF OF WORK

Mine it.

Public mining is scheduled to open on 1 November 2026, 15:42 UTC. People on the waiting list are scheduled to get access 24 hours ahead, from 31 October 2026, 15:42 UTC.

Each block pays 6.25 SWM: 80% to its miner and 20% to the three published project addresses. Mining costs electricity; no coins are promised.

BEFORE YOU START

Choose the right wallet.

SWARM Wallet holds native coins on the SWARM chain. Its keys stay on your device. Tokens bought on Base, BNB Smart Chain or Solana stay on those networks and use a compatible wallet for that network.

The bridge is not live. Buying a token does not move it into your native SWARM Wallet.

BEFORE YOU CONFIRM

Four checks. Every swap.

01

Add SWM by its address.

Paste the address from the token page into your wallet or swap screen. Do not pick a token by name alone; copies can use the same name and logo.

02

Check the network.

Base and BNB Smart Chain share the address; Solana has its own mint. The network selected in your wallet determines which pool you reach.

03

Check the amount received.

Set a slippage limit and read the quoted amount before confirming. Small pools mean price impact. If a swap fails, consider a smaller amount instead of widening the limit.

04

Keep enough for fees.

Swaps use the network’s native coin for fees: ETH on Base, BNB on BNB Smart Chain and SOL on Solana. Check the fee shown by your wallet.

Only the addresses on the token page are ours. We never message you first and never ask for recovery words, private keys or a payment.

SWARM operates an automated trader on the project’s pools. It does not defend a price level or promise what SWM is worth.

REDEMPTION · NOT LIVE

Moving tokens to SWARM mainnet.

A bridge between the token networks and native SWARM is built and under review. It is not live, and the project currently redeems no tokens. Instructions and a public reserves route will be published when it opens.
