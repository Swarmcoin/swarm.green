What is SWARM (SWM)?

SWARM (ticker SWM) is a privacy coin: a cryptocurrency with its own proof-of-work blockchain, on which payments can be shielded so that sender, receiver and amount stay encrypted on-chain. Its supply is capped at 20,999,987.3152 SWM, there was no premine and no sale, and its mainnet has been live since 26 September 2026.

How SWARM keeps a payment private

SWARM has two kinds of payment. A shielded payment uses zero-knowledge proofs: the chain records that a valid payment happened without recording who paid whom or how much, so sender, receiver and amount stay encrypted on-chain. A transparent payment is public, as on most blockchains.

SWARM Wallet gives you a shielded address by default and tells you which kind of payment you are about to make. Shielding protects what is written to the chain. It does not protect everything about how you use the network: your own network connection, for example, is a separate matter.

How SWM is issued

SWM is issued only by mining. A block is found about every 75 seconds and pays 6.25 SWM; the reward halves every 1,680,000 blocks, about every 4 years, so the supply approaches 20,999,987.3152 SWM and never exceeds it. The genesis block holds no spendable coins: there was no premine, no sale and no head start.

Every block reward is split the same way for the whole schedule: 80% to the miner who found the block and 20% to three published project addresses (8% Core Development, 4% Grants & Ecosystem and 8% Community & Development Reserve). Those shares are paid block by block, in public, and the percentages cannot be changed. The full schedule is on the network page; the three addresses and how their keys are held are on the verify page.

Proof of work

SWARM is secured by proof of work, Equihash 200,9, left unchanged from the open-source code it builds on. Specialised hardware for this proof of work exists and nothing in the rules keeps larger miners out.

What it is built on

SWARM is built on proven, open-source code that has secured real money for years. The consensus rules and the cryptography are left unmodified; SWARM adds its own network, its economics and its apps. The code is public at github.com/louisinthesubway/swarm-releases (opens in a new tab): read it, build it, check it.

The software

Everything you need is published with its SHA-256 checksum in the Ecosystem: SWARM Wallet (Windows, macOS, Linux and Android), SWARM Messenger (Windows, macOS and Linux) and SWARM Browser (Windows). The mainnet block explorer is at mainnet.explore.swarm.green (opens in a new tab). The roadmap separates what is live from what is only planned.

Questions

Short answers.

Is SWARM a privacy coin?

Yes. SWARM is a cryptocurrency whose payments can be shielded: sender, receiver and amount stay encrypted on-chain, using zero-knowledge proofs. It runs on its own proof-of-work blockchain, and its ticker is SWM.

When did SWARM launch?

SWARM mainnet went live on 26 September 2026, 12:25 UTC. Block 1 was mined in public from a genesis block that holds no spendable coins.

How many SWM will there be?

At most 20,999,987.3152 SWM. Each block pays 6.25 SWM, and the reward halves every 1,680,000 blocks, about every 4 years.

Was there a premine, a sale or an airdrop?

No. Every SWM in existence was mined. There was no premine, no sale, no presale and no token offering. Anything claiming to be a SWARM sale, presale or airdrop is not the project.

What does a shielded payment hide?

The sender, the receiver and the amount. The chain records that a valid payment happened, not who paid whom or how much.

Is every SWARM payment private?

No. Privacy is a choice you make per payment: shielded payments are encrypted on-chain, transparent payments are public, and the wallet tells you which kind you are about to make.

How do I get SWM?

SWM is issued only by mining; you can receive it from someone who already has it. The project does not sell SWM, does not set a price and does not run an exchange. SWM has no guaranteed value and can lose value, including all of it.

Where do block rewards go?

80% Miner · 8% Core Development · 4% Grants & Ecosystem · 8% Community & Development Reserve. The split is fixed in the genesis rules for the whole emission schedule and is visible in every block.

Who runs SWARM?

A founding team builds the software in the open. The network itself is run by whoever runs a node and mines blocks: there is no company server, no admin key and no account to apply for.

How do I check that I am on the real SWARM chain?

A chain is identified by its genesis block. SWARM mainnet’s genesis hash is 01c34428b9e67cdd8345e0b365aaa37dd8d2d65d3869e0e5d77d567f2c39afdd. The verify page lists the launch facts, the public endpoints and the published addresses.

Which sources are official?

This site, @swarm_coin on X (opens in a new tab), the email address swarmofficial@atomicmail.io and the code at github.com/louisinthesubway/swarm-releases (opens in a new tab). The project will never ask for your recovery words, private keys or a payment.
