What is SWARM, and what is SWM?

SWARM is a proof-of-work network and an open-source software project. SWM is its native currency. The chain targets a block every 75 seconds, begins with a 6.25 SWM block reward, and halves that reward every 1,680,000 blocks. Its nominal cap is 21 million; integer rounding gives an emission ceiling of 20,999,987.3152 SWM. Read the network rules.

How private are SWARM payments?

Shielded native payments encrypt sender, receiver and amount on-chain using zero-knowledge proofs. Transparent payments do not. Shielding does not hide everything about your device or network connection. SWM trading on Base, BNB Smart Chain and Solana takes place on public chains and does not provide native shielded-payment privacy.

How does the closed start work?

SWARM mainnet began on 2 October 2026. Its genesis header time is 15:41:37 UTC; the genesis contains no spendable coins. For the first 29 days, only project machines mine. Waiting-list access is scheduled for 31 October 2026 at 15:42 UTC, followed by public mining on 1 November 2026 at 15:42 UTC. The project receives the mining rewards during its exclusive period, in addition to the published fund allocations. Inspect the genesis and launch details.

Where do the block rewards go?

80% goes to the miner, 8% to Core Development, 4% to Grants & Ecosystem, and 8% to the Community & Development Reserve. The funds are project-controlled destinations published with the launch manifest. The schedule halves the amounts together; late-era rounding retires the small streams first. See the full emission schedule and allocation rules.

Which SWM token addresses are official?

On Base and BNB Smart Chain, the official address is 0xf904C14d21bEF5b8a5345a666C77C9cc2A24043B. On Solana, the official mint is 9Fyen71zwaRs3QfUCsyUooTAkHfNqzUE17xbXwZL6qiH. Reported token supply is updated by an oracle; see the token details for limitations. These tokens are distinct from native SWM. Check the contracts, supply explanation and bridge status.

Can I move SWM between these networks?

The native bridge is built and under review, but it is not live. The project currently provides no token redemption into native SWM. Do not send assets across networks as though an address or ticker makes them interchangeable. Refer to the token page for current bridge information.

Is staking on FUEL part of SWARM mining?

No. SWARM uses proof of work. FUEL is an external service offering its own staking experience; it is not SWARM’s consensus mechanism. Read FUEL’s current supported assets, terms and risks before participating. There is no guaranteed return.

Where can I find official releases and support?

Start at Downloads for release files and checksums, or inspect the source at github.com/Swarmcoin. Contact swarmofficial@atomicmail.io or follow @swarm_coin. We will never ask for your recovery words or private keys.
