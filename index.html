How it works

Shielded by default in the wallet. Transparent by choice.

Shielded transactions keep sender, receiver and amount encrypted on-chain, using zero-knowledge proofs. The SWARM Wallet sends shielded by default; you can choose to reveal a payment.

Shielded by default in the wallet

The SWARM Wallet sends shielded unless you say otherwise. A shielded transaction proves it is valid without publishing who paid whom, or how much.

Reveal when you choose

Send a transparent payment when you want one — for an audit, an exchange, a receipt. Transparent transactions are published like any public chain, and everything that shows that state is Clear Blue, never orange.

Mined, never minted

Every coin has to be found by doing proof of work. The genesis block contains no spendable coins, and a mining reward matures for 100 blocks before it can be spent.

One switch

What the world sees: nothing.

A shielded transaction proves it is valid without revealing who paid whom, or how much. An explorer can see that a block was mined — and that is all.

The Hive

A hive is just a lot of small jobs, done honestly.

SWARM borrows its shape from bees, because a beehive is the clearest picture of what a peer-to-peer network actually is: no boss, no central server, thousands of small contributions adding up to something none of them could manage alone.

Bees

Everyone running a node. Each one keeps its own copy of the rules and checks every block for itself, so nobody has to be trusted.

Foragers

The miners. They spend real electricity on proof of work to secure the chain. The work is Equihash — CPU-mineable on the testnet — so ordinary computers can forage from the first block. Nothing in the rules keeps bigger miners out later.

Honey

The block reward. 6.25 SWM for every block found, paid for real work — never minted for anyone in advance.

Honeycomb

The blockchain. One cell for each block, sealed in order, each one referring back to the last — a shared ledger anyone can re-check.

The Hive

The peer-to-peer network itself. There is no central server to ask permission from. The more bees run nodes, the harder the hive is to switch off.

Why SWARM

Old-fashioned ideas about money, built on proven code.

  • Private by choice

    Every payment can be transparent or shielded. Shielded transactions keep sender, receiver and amount encrypted on-chain, using zero-knowledge proofs. You decide, payment by payment.

  • Run by its community

    SWARM is developed in the open by its founding team and contributors. The network itself is run by whoever runs nodes and mines blocks — nobody needs permission to join.

  • Sound, predictable supply

    6.25 SWM a block, halving every 1,680,000 blocks — about four years — up to a maximum of 20,999,987.3152 coins. The schedule is fixed in the code and published in full.

  • Proven foundations

    SWARM is built by forking open-source Zcash software — the Zebra full node, the Zaino indexer and the Zingo desktop wallet — with consensus rules and cryptography left unmodified.

  • Open source

    The node, the indexer and the wallet are all open source. Read the code, build it yourself, and check that it does what this page says it does.

Not affiliated with anyone. SWARM is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Electric Coin Company, the Zcash Foundation, Zingo Labs, Foursquare’s Swarm app, or the Ethereum Swarm (BZZ) project.

Mining

Every worker feeds the hive.

The proof of work is Equihash, inherited from the Zcash software SWARM forks and left unmodified. On the testnet it is CPU-mineable, so SWARM is open to ordinary computers from the first block. There is no hardware gate — and no promise that home computers stay competitive forever. As difficulty rises, professional and specialised miners can join, and nothing in the rules keeps anyone out at either end.

Three things happen when you mine

  1. 01Install SWARM Node on a computer you already own.
  2. 02Choose the address your rewards are paid to. A mining reward arrives transparent and matures for 100 blocks.
  3. 03Press Start. Mining only ever runs while you leave it running, and stops the moment you press Stop.

Consent-first: nothing runs hidden, and nothing starts by itself.

Supply

Do the work. Earn the honey.

The monetary base is inherited from the Zcash design and is not up for negotiation: a 75-second target block time, 6.25 SWM a block, a halving every 1,680,000 blocks, and a hard ceiling of 20,999,987.3152 SWM. No premine, no hidden treasury — every allocation is visible in every block.

Where each block reward goes

In every block, for the whole emission schedule. Each halving halves all four amounts together.

Of every block reward: 80% to the miner, 8% to Core Development, 4% to Grants & Ecosystem and 8% to the Community & Development Reserve. In era 0 that is 5.00, 0.50, 0.25 and 0.50 SWM.

  • MinerPaid to the forager who found the block. 5.00 SWM per block in era 0. 80%
  • Core DevelopmentFunds work on the node, indexer and wallet. 0.50 SWM per block in era 0. 8%
  • Grants & EcosystemFunds tooling, documentation and community work. 0.25 SWM per block in era 0. 4%
  • Community & Development ReserveSet aside for the community and for future development. How it is governed and spent will be defined separately and published before any mainnet. 0.50 SWM per block in era 0. 8%

Fixed in the genesis rules for the whole emission schedule. Paid block by block as part of each block reward — not a premine.

At every halving

The block reward halves and the 80 / 8 / 4 / 8 split stays exactly the same. The percentages are fixed in the genesis rules and cannot be changed.

Over the whole life of the chain that is 80% to miners, 8% to Core Development, 4% to Grants & Ecosystem and 8% to the Community & Development Reserve.

The reserve

Paid block by block to its own predefined address, like the other allocations. How it is governed and spent will be defined separately and published before any mainnet.

Roadmap

No dates. Just the order of things.

Each stage has to be finished before the next one starts. We would rather be late than wrong about money.

  1. Now

    Private testnet

    Node, indexer and wallet running end to end on a closed testnet.

  2. Next

    Public testnet

    An open testnet with a block explorer and a faucet, so anyone can join and try it.

  3. Then

    Independent security review

    An external review of the changes made on top of the upstream Zcash software.

  4. Finally

    Mainnet readiness decision

    A public decision, made only once the work above is done. There is no mainnet date.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is SWARM live?

SWARM is on testnet only. There is no mainnet, no sale, no token offering and no mainnet date.

What is the coin called?

The coin is SWARM and its ticker is SWM. Every amount on this site is written in SWM.

On the testnet these are test coins. They have no monetary value.

Do test coins have value?

No. Test coins have no monetary value. They exist so the software can be exercised and broken safely.

How does SWARM relate to Zcash?

SWARM is built by forking open-source Zcash software: the Zebra full node, the Zaino indexer and the Zingo desktop wallet. The consensus rules and the cryptography are left unmodified.

SWARM is an independent network. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Electric Coin Company, the Zcash Foundation or Zingo Labs.

Can I mine with a normal PC?

On the testnet, yes. The proof of work is Equihash and it is CPU-mineable on the testnet, so SWARM is open to ordinary computers from the first block.

There is no promise that home computers stay competitive forever. As the network grows and difficulty rises, professional and specialised miners can join — and nothing in the rules keeps anyone out, at either end.

SWARM Node will let you start and stop mining with one click — it only ever runs when you press Start, and it stops when you press Stop.

Is there a premine?

No. The genesis block contains no spendable coins. Every coin has to be mined, and mining rewards mature after 100 blocks before they can be spent.

How private is it?

Privacy is a choice you make per payment. Shielded transactions keep sender, receiver and amount encrypted on-chain, using zero-knowledge proofs. Transparent transactions do not.

The SWARM Wallet sends shielded by default; you can choose to reveal a payment.

Shielding protects what is written to the chain. It does not protect everything about how you use the network — for example, your own network connection is a separate matter.

What does the map on this page show?

Only the nodes we know about that share a location, at city level. Today that is one: the project’s own seed server in Dallas, United States.

Sharing a location will be opt-in in SWARM Node, and it will never be finer than a city. The map is not a count of the network — it is a count of the nodes that chose to appear on it.

Where do block rewards go?

Every block reward is split 80% to the miner who found the block, 8% to Core Development, 4% to Grants & Ecosystem and 8% to the Community & Development Reserve. That split runs for the whole emission schedule: each halving reduces all four amounts together and the structure never changes.

Each allocation is paid automatically to its predefined destination address. Destinations can only be changed through a formal protocol upgrade; the percentages themselves cannot be changed.

No premine, no hidden treasury — every allocation is visible in every block. The full numbers are on the network page.

Is this related to Ethereum Swarm or the Foursquare Swarm app?

No. SWARM has no connection to the Ethereum Swarm (BZZ) project or to Foursquare’s Swarm app. The logo is an original geometric design.

How do I reach the project?

Write to swarmofficial@atomicmail.io or follow @swarm_coin on X (opens in a new tab). Together with this site and our GitHub, those are the only official channels.

We will never ask for your recovery words, private keys or a payment — not by email, not on X, not anywhere.
