What SWARM is

A currency, a network, and the software to run both.

SWARM is a new cryptocurrency, ticker SWM, with its own blockchain. It is secured by proof of work, its supply is fixed in the code, and every payment can be shielded so that sender, receiver and amount stay encrypted on-chain. It is built on proven, open-source code that has been securing real money for years, with the consensus rules and cryptography left exactly as they are.

The money

6.25 SWM per block, a block every 75 seconds, a halving every 1,680,000 blocks and a ceiling of 20,999,987.3152 SWM. No premine: the genesis block holds nothing, so every coin is mined. Of each block reward 80% goes to the miner and 20% to three published project destinations, block by block, for the life of the chain.

The software

SWARM Node runs a full node and mines with one click. SWARM Wallet holds, sends and receives, shielded or transparent, on desktop and phone. A public block explorer for mainnet is live. All of it is open source: read it, build it, check it.

The people

A founding team builds the software in the open. The network itself belongs to whoever runs a node and mines a block: there is no company server, no admin key, no account to apply for. The rules every node enforces are the same for the founders as for anyone else.

The privacy

Shielded transactions use zero-knowledge proofs, so the chain records that a valid payment happened without recording who paid whom or how much. Transparent payments exist too, and every wallet says plainly which kind you are about to make.

The mining

Proof of work is Equihash, with a proven per-block difficulty rule left unchanged. The chain starts at minimum difficulty, so an ordinary computer finds blocks from the first day. Mining runs only when you press Start. As the network grows, difficulty rises and larger miners can join; nothing in the rules keeps anyone out.

The principles

Reuse proven code; never reinvent consensus or cryptography. Publish every rule, address and launch input before genesis. Nothing runs on your machine without your consent. No sale, no premine, no head start, no hidden treasury: every allocation is visible in every block.

The network, live

SWARM mainnet is running: blocks every 75 seconds, 6.25 SWM each, the 80 / 8 / 4 / 8 split paid in every one of them. Block 1 was mined in public at launch from a genesis block that holds nothing; the genesis hash, the three destination addresses and the launch manifest are on the network page so anyone can check they are on the real chain.

What comes next

The coin and the network are the foundation. On top of them: SWARM Market, an online marketplace where you pay merchants in SWM; a privacy browser with the wallet built in; and private messaging, end-to-end encrypted, with payments inside the conversation.

The Hive

A hive is just a lot of small jobs, done honestly.

SWARM borrows its shape from bees, because a beehive is the clearest picture of what a peer-to-peer network actually is: no boss, no central server, thousands of small contributions adding up to something none of them could manage alone.

Bees

Everyone running a node. Each one keeps its own copy of the rules and checks every block for itself, so nobody has to be trusted.

Foragers

The miners. They spend real electricity on proof of work to secure the chain. The work is Equihash and the chain starts at minimum difficulty, so ordinary computers can forage from the first block. Nothing in the rules keeps bigger miners out later.

Honey

The block reward. 6.25 SWM for every block found, paid for real work — never minted for anyone in advance.

Honeycomb

The blockchain. One cell for each block, sealed in order, each one referring back to the last — a shared ledger anyone can re-check.

The Hive

The peer-to-peer network itself. There is no central server to ask permission from. The more bees run nodes, the harder the hive is to switch off.

Shielded

Privacy, by choice. Shielded transactions keep sender, receiver and amount encrypted on-chain, using zero-knowledge proofs.

Why SWARM

Old-fashioned ideas about money, built on proven code.

  • Run by its community

    SWARM is developed in the open by its founding team and contributors. The network itself is run by whoever runs nodes and mines blocks — nobody needs permission to join, and the founders play by the same rules as everyone else.

  • Sound, predictable supply

    6.25 coins a block, halving every 1,680,000 blocks — about four years — up to a maximum of 20,999,987.3152 coins. The schedule is fixed in the code and published in full.

  • Proven foundations

    SWARM is built on proven, open-source code that has secured real money for years. The consensus rules and the cryptography are left unmodified; SWARM adds its network, its economics and its apps on top.

  • Open source

    The node, the indexer and the wallet are all open source. Read the code, build it yourself, and check that it does what this page says it does.

Where each block reward goes

In every block, for the whole emission schedule. Each halving halves all four amounts together.

Of every block reward: 80% to the miner, 8% to Core Development, 4% to Grants & Ecosystem and 8% to the Community & Development Reserve. In era 0 that is 5.00, 0.50, 0.25 and 0.50 coins.

  • MinerPaid to the forager who found the block. 5.00 coins per block in era 0. 80%
  • Core DevelopmentFunds work on the node, indexer and wallet. 0.50 coins per block in era 0. 8%
  • Grants & EcosystemFunds tooling, documentation and community work. 0.25 coins per block in era 0. 4%
  • Community & Development ReserveSet aside for the community and for future development. Governed and spent under the published custody policy. 0.50 coins per block in era 0. 8%

Fixed in the genesis rules for the whole emission schedule. Paid block by block as part of each block reward — not a premine.

At every halving

The block reward halves and the 80 / 8 / 4 / 8 split stays exactly the same. The percentages are fixed in the genesis rules and cannot be changed.

Over the whole life of the chain that is 80% to miners, 8% to Core Development, 4% to Grants & Ecosystem and 8% to the Community & Development Reserve.

In the last eras integer rounding retires the small streams first: Grants & Ecosystem rounds to zero from era 25, Core Development and the Reserve from era 26, and the miner keeps the remainder.

The reserve

Paid block by block to its own predefined address, like the other allocations. How it is governed and spent is set out in the published custody policy, linked from the network page.

Roadmap

The money first. Then the things you do with it.

The order is fixed; the calendar follows the work.

  1. Live

    SWARM mainnet

    The coin, the chain and the apps to run them. Block 1 was mined in public from a genesis block that holds nothing; every SWM since has been mined.

    You can

    • mine with one click in SWARM Node
    • hold, send and receive SWM, shielded or transparent, in SWARM Wallet
    • check the genesis hash and every block for yourself — the node verifies, it never trusts
  2. Now

    Growing the swarm

    More bees, more cities, more ways in. The wallet goes to the App Store and Google Play, every build ships signed, and more seed nodes come online around the world.

    You can

    • run a node and light up your city on the map
    • install the wallet on your phone
    • tell us what breaks
    • browse the chain in the mainnet block explorer
  3. Next

    SWARM Market

    An online marketplace where merchants list what they sell and buyers pay in SWM, shielded by default. The wallet checks every invoice before you confirm; the Market never holds your coins; fees are shown before you pay; order details never touch the chain.

    You can

    • pay for real goods and services with SWM
    • sell to the whole swarm without a payment processor
    • keep what you bought between you and the seller
  4. Then

    Privacy browser

    A browser with the SWARM wallet built in. Paying a site is one click with the same confirmation screen as the wallet; trackers are blocked, fingerprinting is reduced, nothing phones home, and each site gets only the permissions you give it.

    You can

    • pay from the address bar
    • browse without being followed
    • give a site access to your wallet only when you choose
  5. Then

    Private messaging

    End-to-end encrypted messages between people who hold SWARM wallets, with payments inside the conversation. Your chat identity is separate from your spending key, relays see only what they need to deliver, and no message is ever written to the chain.

    You can

    • message and pay in one place
    • send an invoice in a chat
    • verify a contact once and be told if their key ever changes

No dates. Each product ships when it is finished and reviewed, and is announced here first.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is SWARM live?

Yes. SWARM mainnet is running. You can download the wallet and the node today, mine blocks and send shielded payments. The genesis hash, the launch manifest and the three destination addresses are on the network page, so you can check that the chain you join is the one that was announced.

There was no sale, no presale and no token offering. Every SWM in existence was mined.

What is SWM worth?

Whatever people agree it is worth. SWARM does not set a price, promise one, or run an exchange. Like any cryptocurrency, SWM can lose value, including all of it. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

Can I mine with a normal PC?

Yes. The proof of work is Equihash and the chain started at minimum difficulty, so SWARM is open to ordinary computers from the first block.

There is no promise that home computers stay competitive forever. As the network grows and difficulty rises, professional and specialised miners can join — and nothing in the rules keeps anyone out, at either end. Equihash hardware already exists, so that moment can come early. It is a consequence of keeping the rules open, and we say so rather than pretend otherwise.

SWARM Node lets you start and stop mining with one click. It only ever runs when you press Start, and it stops when you press Stop.

Is there a premine?

No. The genesis block contains no spendable coins. Every coin has to be mined, and mining rewards mature after 100 blocks before they can be spent.

How was the launch kept fair?

Three ways, all checkable. The genesis block contains no spendable coins, so nobody started with a balance. The genesis was derived from a public, unpredictable input fixed only at launch, so nobody could mine blocks in private beforehand. And the rules, the three destination addresses and the launch manifest were published before block 1, so anyone can verify that the chain they are syncing is the one that was announced.

The 20% of each block reward that goes to the three project destinations is not a premine and not hidden: it is paid block by block, in public, for the whole life of the chain, and the percentages cannot be changed.

Where do block rewards go?

Every block reward is split 80% to the miner who found the block, 8% to Core Development, 4% to Grants & Ecosystem and 8% to the Community & Development Reserve. That split runs for the whole emission schedule: each halving reduces all four amounts together and the structure never changes.

Each allocation is paid automatically to its predefined destination address. Destinations can only be changed through a formal protocol upgrade; the percentages themselves cannot be changed.

In the last eras integer rounding retires the small streams first: Grants & Ecosystem rounds to zero from era 25, Core Development and the Reserve from era 26, and the miner keeps the remainder.

No premine, no hidden treasury — every allocation is visible in every block. The full numbers are on the network page.

Who controls the three project destinations?

The project does, under the custody policy published with the launch manifest: keys generated offline, held on separate devices under a threshold policy, with the spending and recovery procedure rehearsed before the addresses were fixed into the rules. It is custody of the project’s share, not a governance vote: nothing can change the percentages.

How private is it?

Privacy is a choice you make per payment. Shielded transactions keep sender, receiver and amount encrypted on-chain, using zero-knowledge proofs. Transparent transactions do not, and the wallet tells you which kind you are about to make.

Shielding protects what is written to the chain. It does not protect everything about how you use the network — for example, your own network connection is a separate matter.

What is coming after the coin?

SWARM Market, an online marketplace where you pay merchants in SWM; a privacy browser with the wallet built in; and private, end-to-end encrypted messaging with payments inside the conversation. In that order, each when it is finished and reviewed. The roadmap has the detail.

How do I reach the project?

Write to swarmofficial@atomicmail.io or follow @swarm_coin on X (opens in a new tab). Together with this site and our GitHub, those are the only official channels. Anything else claiming to be SWARM — a sale, a presale, an airdrop — is not us.

We will never ask for your recovery words, private keys or a payment — not by email, not on X, not anywhere.
