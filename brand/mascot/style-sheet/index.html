01 / THE SILHOUETTE

Recognizable from every angle.

Keep the rounded head, tapered striped body, swept antennae and four honeycomb wings. The futuristic details support this familiar shape.

02 / THE SIGNATURE

The screen is the face.

The original three-part SWARM mark becomes raised, illuminated pixels on a dark screen. Keep the honey upper shape, orange lower shapes and visible gaps between tiles.

  • Preserve the mark’s proportions and negative space.
  • Give the pixels depth and a controlled warm glow.
  • Let the whole face respond with a gentle tilt and light.
  • Keep every pixel distinct when the logo enlarges.

03

Engineered details.

Small warm status lights, precise seams and a compact chest core. Keep the soft bee character in charge.

04 / MOVEMENT & EXPRESSION

Alive, with restraint.

Implemented on the website

Full-header pixel gathering, an oval flight through depth, a complete turn and fast wing flutter. The same screen-eye pixels move forward and enlarge into the mark, then disperse. Wing motion and pointer response keep the character alive. Pause and reduced motion remain available.

Direction for future animation

Use a slight head tilt for curiosity, a calm level pose for confidence and a small light sweep for focus. These are expression guidelines, not delivered rigged animation states.

Keep the identity intact

Avoid human eyes, a solid logo sticker, cold neon, heavy armor or an aggressive silhouette. Screen expressions must preserve the recognizable SWARM mark.
